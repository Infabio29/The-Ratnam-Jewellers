Certified, and priced in the open

Jewellery is a big purchase, and many people like to have a piece checked. Our gold jewellery comes with an IGI certificate, or an HRD or GIA one, so there is no doubt about what you are buying.

We are the only store on Amer Road offering jewellery certified by IGI, GIA and HRD. We also explain how each piece is priced, so you can decide with confidence.

IGI

International Gemological Institute

The world’s largest gemological laboratory.

GIA

Gemological Institute of America

An independent grading laboratory for diamonds and coloured stones.

HRD

Hoge Raad voor Diamant

The Antwerp diamond laboratory, also known as HRD Antwerp.

A family house, founded on trust

Rajneesh Gupta has worked with jewellery for more than thirty years. Today two generations of the family work in the business, with one aim: to share the craft and heritage of Indian jewellery, honestly and warmly, with visitors from around the world.

  1. 1994

    Rajneesh Gupta starts working with jewellery, aged 18.

  2. 2007

    He founds The Ratnam Jewellers in Jaipur, in the Pink City.

  3. Today

    A family business with two generations at work.
